Delete Your Benchly Account

You can request deletion of your Benchly account and associated personal data.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Benchly.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Open the account or profile settings section.
  5. Select Delete Account.
  6. Read the confirmation information and confirm the deletion request.

Request Deletion Outside the App

If you cannot access Benchly, you can request account deletion by emailing vladimirjosifoski94@gmail.com with the subject Benchly Account Deletion Request.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Benchly account whenever possible. We may need to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

What Is Deleted

Subject to applicable legal and security requirements, deleting your account will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, such as account and profile information.

Content that is part of the shared Benchly community may require special handling. Before launch, Benchly should clearly define whether user-submitted benches, photos, memories, comments, and reports are deleted, anonymized, or retained after account deletion.

Information We May Retain

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or protect the security and integrity of Benchly.
