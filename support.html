When Contacting Us

To help us investigate quickly, please include:

  • A short description of the issue.
  • Your device model.
  • Your iOS or Android version.
  • The version of Benchly you are using, if known.
  • Steps that reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot or screen recording when useful.

Account Access

If you cannot access your account, contact support from the email address associated with your Benchly account whenever possible.

Report Content or a Bench

If you find inappropriate, inaccurate, unsafe, or abusive content, use the reporting feature available inside Benchly. If you cannot use the in-app reporting flow, contact us by email and include enough information to identify the content.
